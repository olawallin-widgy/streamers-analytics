A proposed format for the weekly 30-minute TG3 review — a working draft for internal review before it goes to the investors. Not a replacement for any existing document; it's the meeting artifact the weekly needs. Built from two inputs.

The two inputs

The Company Direction doc

The strategy spine — streamer & operator acquisition model, tier structure, channel split, 2026 targets. This format reports against it; it doesn't reopen it.

The Amplitude dashboards

The data layer — 9 dashboards across the Members & Streamers funnels. This format is the executive view; the dashboards are the drill-down.

Feedback on the Company Direction doc

What it does well — keep it

  • The streamer & operator acquisition strategy itself: ideal profile, tier mix, channel split, casino pipeline. Solid — this format reports against it, doesn't touch it.
  • Clear 2026 acquisition targets — these become the "vs. plan" line the scorecard reports against.
  • It already proposes a Pulse / Health cadence — adopted below.

What this weekly format adds

  • A meeting artifact, not a strategy doc — a narrative you can run a 30-min discussion from.
  • A "vs. plan" view and clear prioritisation — what matters this week, not everything at once.
  • The full business in one view — the doc is acquisition-only; revenue, member & product performance, and company & operations are added here.
The weekly — 30 minutes
How this works
Every week, this gives a snapshot of the whole business in 30 minutes — where we are, what's driving it, where we're focused, and the state of the company itself. One page, five parts.

This first version is a conceptual report — the format itself is the deliverable. The next step is to populate it with the data we already have, then build it up over time as more tracking comes online and more datapoints become available.

Why the numbers moved
What's driving it (Insights)
The 5–6 things that actually moved the numbers this week — each tagged by area, so nothing quietly gets ignored.
Streamer acquisition
Onboarding mix skewed to Tier 6
Volume held but the Tier 3 pipeline is thin — 1 vs the 2–5/mo plan. Conference recruiting is the lever.
Casino acquisition
Operator pipeline converting slowly
Healthy top-of-pipeline but only 2 in negotiation — the deal-brief standardisation should shorten contract velocity.
Marketing
Streamer-referred traffic is the growth
Organic flat, paid steady; streamer-referred sessions up. Directional for now until referral attribution is in place.
Product
Activation drop-off is in stream discovery
Members register but don't reliably find a live stream to join. A discovery-surface fix is in scope.
Product
Member revenue tracking still partial
The dollar value of FTDs, tips and token purchases isn't fully wired — revenue reads are directional.
Operations
Verification queue is lengthening
Time-to-Live drifted from ~2 to ~3 days — KYC throughput is becoming the constraint on getting new streamers live and earning.
Discipline: product, marketing and operations get a slot here every week — even when streamer acquisition is the loudest story in the room. That's how the format stays a full-business view, not a supply-side one.
